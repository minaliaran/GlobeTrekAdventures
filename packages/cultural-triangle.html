About This Package

Explore the ancient heart of Sri Lanka, visiting Anuradhapura, Polonnaruwa, Sigiriya, and Dambulla. This 8-day journey immerses you in the rich history and heritage of the island. Walk through the ruins of ancient capitals, marvel at towering stupas, and climb the iconic Lion Rock fortress.

Tour Highlights

Climb Sigiriya Rock Fortress
Cycle through Polonnaruwa ruins
Explore Dambulla Cave Temple
Discover Anuradhapura sacred city

Itinerary

Day 1-2: Arrival & Anuradhapura

Arrive in Colombo and transfer to Anuradhapura. Explore the Sri Maha Bodhi, Ruwanwelisaya, and ancient monasteries.

Day 3: Polonnaruwa

Transfer to Polonnaruwa. Rent bicycles and ride through the ancient ruins, visiting the Gal Viharaya and Royal Palace.

Day 4-5: Sigiriya & Minneriya

Early morning climb of Sigiriya Rock Fortress. In the afternoon, enjoy a jeep safari at Minneriya National Park to see the elephant gathering.

Day 6-8: Dambulla & Departure

Visit the Dambulla Cave Temple. Relax at your hotel before transferring back to Colombo for your departure flight.
