About This Package

Relax on the pristine beaches of Mirissa, explore the historic Galle Fort, and enjoy thrilling whale watching excursions. A perfect 7-day escape for beach lovers seeking sun, sand, and coastal culture along Sri Lanka's stunning southern shores.

Itinerary

Day 1-2: Arrival & Galle

Arrive in Colombo and transfer to Galle. Spend your days exploring the cobblestone streets of the UNESCO World Heritage Galle Fort.

Day 3-4: Unawatuna

Relax on the golden sands of Unawatuna Beach. Enjoy beachside dining, swimming, and snorkeling at Jungle Beach.

Day 5-6: Mirissa & Whale Watching

Early morning boat tour from Mirissa harbor to spot majestic blue whales and dolphins. Visit the famous Coconut Tree Hill.

Day 7: Departure

Enjoy one last morning on the beach before your scenic coastal drive back to Colombo for your departure flight.
