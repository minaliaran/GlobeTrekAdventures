About This Package

Hike lush rainforest trails, spot rare endemic birds, and enjoy thrilling river activities with expert local guides. This 5-day adventure into Sri Lanka's wet zone is perfect for nature enthusiasts and adrenaline seekers.

Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival & Kitulgala

Arrive in Colombo and drive straight to Kitulgala, the adventure capital. Check into your eco-lodge by the Kelani River.

Day 2: River Adventures

Experience the thrill of white water rafting down the Kelani River, followed by waterfall abseiling and jungle trekking.

Day 3: Journey to Sinharaja

Travel to the edge of the Sinharaja Forest Reserve, a UNESCO World Heritage site. Enjoy an evening nature walk around the lodge.

Day 4-5: Rainforest Trek & Departure

Full day guided trek through Sinharaja, spotting rare flora and fauna, exotic birds, and monkeys. Depart for Colombo on Day 5.
