About This Package

Embark on thrilling safari rides across Yala and Udawalawe National Parks. Witness majestic wildlife in their natural habitat over 6 unforgettable days, spotting elusive leopards, herds of elephants, and diverse bird species.

Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival & Transfer

Arrive in Colombo and transfer directly to the southern wilderness. Settle into your luxury safari camp bordering Yala National Park.

Day 2-3: Yala National Park

Morning and evening jeep safaris in Yala, with the highest density of leopards in the world. Look out for sloth bears and crocodiles.

Day 4: Bundala & Transfer

Morning bird watching at Bundala National Park. Afternoon transfer to Udawalawe and visit the Elephant Transit Home.

Day 5-6: Udawalawe Safari & Departure

Full day safari in Udawalawe National Park, famous for its large elephant herds. Depart for Colombo on Day 6.
