About This Package

Ride the iconic hill-country train and explore lush tea estates, misty viewpoints, and cool mountain towns. This 6-day journey through the central highlands offers breathtaking scenery and a refreshing climate.

Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival & Kandy

Arrive in Colombo and drive to Kandy. Visit the Temple of the Tooth Relic and enjoy a traditional cultural dance show in the evening.

Day 2: Nuwara Eliya

Drive up into the mountains to 'Little England'. Visit a working tea factory and stroll around Gregory Lake.

Day 3-4: Ella Train Ride & Exploration

Take the famous scenic train ride from Nanu Oya to Ella. Hike Little Adam's Peak and visit the Nine Arch Bridge.

Day 5-6: Haputale & Departure

Visit Lipton's Seat for panoramic views before making the scenic descent back to Colombo for your departure.
