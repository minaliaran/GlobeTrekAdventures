About This Package

A tranquil 5-day getaway to the untouched beaches of the east coast. Perfect for families looking to relax by the lagoon, enjoy calm watersports, and discover the beauty of Sri Lanka's pristine eastern shoreline.

Tour Highlights

Relax at Pasikuda Beach

Trincomalee Harbor Tour
Calm Lagoon Watersports

Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival & Transfer

Arrive in Colombo and take a scenic drive to the east coast. Check into your beachfront resort in Pasikuda.

Day 2: Pasikuda Beach Day

Enjoy a full day of relaxation on the calm, shallow waters of Pasikuda Bay. Perfect for swimming and family fun.

Day 3: Trincomalee Exploration

Travel to Trincomalee. Visit the Koneswaram Temple and enjoy the panoramic views from Swami Rock.

Day 4-5: Pigeon Island & Departure

Take a boat ride to Pigeon Island National Park for world-class snorkeling before departing back to Colombo.
