Pasikuda Beach

Walk for miles into the crystal-clear, waist-deep waters of one of the world's longest stretches of shallow coastline.

An Eastern Gem

The Shallow Blue Haven

Pasikuda Beach is legendary for its semi-circular bay and incredible shallow waters. Due to its unique geography, the ocean remains only waist-deep for nearly 200 meters from the shore, making it arguably the safest and most relaxing swimming spot in all of Sri Lanka.

This pristine stretch of white sand on the east coast is lined with some of the island's most luxurious resorts. Whether you're interested in snorkeling among the coral reefs further out, enjoying a variety of water sports, or simply wading through the gentle, turquoise waves, Pasikuda offers an unparalleled beach experience defined by serenity and natural beauty.

Quick Facts

  • Best Season:
    May to September offers the calmest seas and best weather on the east coast.
  • Safety:
    Extremely safe for children and non-swimmers due to the long shallow shelf.
  • Temperature:
    The water in the bay is consistently warm and inviting year-round.
