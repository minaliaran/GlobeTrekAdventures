About the Destination

Pasikuda is a stunning coastal destination on Sri Lanka's eastern shores, famous for its shallow, crystal-clear waters and long stretches of soft golden sand.

Known as one of the safest beaches for swimming, its calm seas extend far into the ocean, making it ideal for relaxation and family-friendly experiences. With its peaceful atmosphere and vibrant turquoise coastline, Pasikuda offers a perfect tropical escape.
