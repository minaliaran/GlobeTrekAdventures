Coconut Cultural Park

Discover the historical and cultural significance of the coconut tree, known as the "tree of life" in Sri Lanka's eastern province.

Learning & Nature

The Sacred Grove of the East

Located in the heart of the Pasikuda tourism zone, the Coconut Cultural Park is a unique initiative by the Coconut Cultivation Board. It serves as both a museum and a garden, educating visitors on the hundreds of uses of the coconut tree, from its culinary importance to its essential role in traditional handicrafts and construction.

The park is a beautifully landscaped green space where visitors can see ancient coconut varieties, observe traditional coconut sap extraction, and learn about the entire lifecycle of this vital plant. It provides a peaceful and educational escape from the sun-drenched beaches, offering a deeper connection to the local culture and the sustainable heritage of the island's eastern lands.
