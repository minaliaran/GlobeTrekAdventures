Batticaloa Lagoon

Discover the vast, serene waters of the island's largest lagoon, famous for its historic "singing fish" and unique eastern charm.

Watery Wonders

The Gentle Pulse of the East

Batticaloa Lagoon is the largest in Sri Lanka, a complex network of estuarine waters that stretch for over 50 kilometers. It is the lifeblood of the eastern province, providing a rich habitat for wildlife and a vital source of livelihood for thousands of fishing families who still use traditional rowing boats.

The lagoon is legendary for its "singing fish," a phenomenon heard during moonlit nights near the Kallady Bridge, where rhythmic musical sounds emanate from the waters. A boat ride through the lagoon's mangroves and under its historic bridges offers a glimpse into the serene, rhythmic life of the eastern coast, far from the bustling tourist trails.
