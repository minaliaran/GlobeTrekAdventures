About Kandy

As the last capital of the ancient kings, Kandy is a city of immense spiritual and historical power. Nestled amongst rolling green hills, it wraps around a serene central lake, offering a cool refuge from the tropical heat.

Whether you're witnessing the grandeur of the Esala Perahera or wandering through the lush orchids of Peradeniya, Kandy invites you to step into a world where tradition is still very much alive.
