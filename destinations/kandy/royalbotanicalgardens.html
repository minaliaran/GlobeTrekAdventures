Royal Botanical Gardens

The largest and most influential botanical garden in Sri Lanka, home to over 4,000 species of tropical flora.

Flora & Nature

The Jewel of Peradeniya

Established in 1821, the Royal Botanical Gardens in Peradeniya are situated just a few kilometers from Kandy town, beautifully bordered by the Mahaweli River. These gardens were once the pleasure gardens of the Kandyan royalty.

The garden is most famous for its extraordinary collection of orchids, its giant Java fig tree, and the iconic avenue of royal palms. It is a peaceful escape for families, researchers, and tourists looking for the tranquility of nature.

Visitor Information

    07:30 AM - 10:30 AM before the midday heat and larger crowds.
  • Must See:
    The Orchid House, The Cannonball Tree, and the Great Lawn.
  • Location:
    Peradeniya, Kandy (approx. 15 min drive from Kandy town).
  • Photography:
    Unlimited photography allowed. The palm avenue is a top spot!
