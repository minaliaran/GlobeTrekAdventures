Temple of the Tooth

The sacred Sri Dalada Maligawa, the most revered Buddhist site in Sri Lanka, housing the tooth relic of Lord Buddha.

Spiritual Heart

The Sacred Relic

Located within the royal palace complex of the former Kingdom of Kandy, the Temple of the Tooth is a beacon of Buddhist heritage. Since ancient times, the relic has played an important role in local politics because it is believed that whoever holds the relic holds the governance of the country.

The temple is a masterpiece of Kandyan architecture, featuring white walls with carved openings, golden canopies, and intricate wood carvings. Visitors can witness daily 'Thevava' (rituals) accompanied by traditional drumming and explore the various museums within the complex.

Visitor Information

  • Dress Code:
    Must cover shoulders and knees. White attire is preferred as a sign of respect.
  • Best Time:
    During Puja hours (05:30 AM, 09:30 AM, 06:30 PM) to see the golden casket.
  • Entry Fee:
    approx. 2,000 LKR (Foreign). Locals are free.
  • Footwear:
    Shoes must be removed and stored at the entrance for a small fee.
