Royal Forest Sanctuary

Udawattakele Forest

The historic 'Garden Above the Palace,' an ancient forest reserve situated behind the Temple of the Tooth.

Nature & History

The Hidden Sanctuary

Udawattakele served as the royal pleasure garden for the Kandyan kings and was declared a forest reserve in 1856. It is rich in biodiversity and history, containing several ancient caves used by monks for meditation.

The reserve has a well-marked network of paths that are perfect for hiking and bird watching. It's a peaceful sanctuary just minutes away from the city's hustle, offering cool shade and the chance to see various species of trees and monkeys.

Explorer Tips

  • Duration:
    A full loop takes about 1.5 - 2 hours. Easy forest trails.
  • Must See:
    The lovers' walk, the forest pond, and the ancient ironwood forest.
  • Opening Hours:
    07:00 AM - 05:00 PM. Best visited in the morning.
  • Safety Tip:
    Monkeys are active; keep food and shiny objects packed away.
