The Heart of Kandy

Kandy Lake, or 'Bogambara Lake,' was created in 1807 by King Sri Wickrama Rajasinghe, the last ruler of the Kingdom of Kandy. It is surrounded by the Temple of the Tooth and a beautiful walkway lined with palm trees.

The small island in the center was once used as a personal getaway by the royal harem. Today, it is a serene spot where locals and tourists stroll in the evenings, watching the golden reflections of the setting sun on the water.

Activity Tips

  • Best Activity:
    A full walk around the 3.2km perimeter at sunset (approx. 45-60 mins).
  • Boat Rides:
    Small boat tours are available from the pier near the Temple.
  • Bird Watching:
    The lake is home to numerous water birds and monitors.
  • Safety Tip:
    High walls line most of the lake. Be careful near the edge.
