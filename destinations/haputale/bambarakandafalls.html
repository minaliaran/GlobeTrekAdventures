Sri Lanka's Highest

Bambarakanda Falls

The tallest waterfall in the country, a magnificent thin veil of water plunging 263 meters through the pine forests of the Kalupahana valley.

High Altitude Wonders

The Vertical Veil in the Mist

Bambarakanda Falls takes the crown as the highest waterfall in Sri Lanka, standing at 263 meters. Surrounded by scenic pine forests and rugged hills, it offers a dramatic sight as the water plumes down the sheer vertical rock cliff. The area is much quieter than the more famous Ella or Kandy, making it a perfect escape for nature seekers.

Visitors can hike through the surrounding forests to reach the base of the falls, where the cool mountain spray provides a refreshing retreat. For the more adventurous, several forest trails lead to the top of the falls and nearby viewpoints, offering panoramic vistas of the southern hills and the picturesque Belihuloya region.

Quick Facts

  • Height:
    263 meters (863 feet), ranked 299th in the world.
  • Location:
    Kalupahana, off the A5 highway between Belihuloya and Haputale.
  • Best Season:
    Visited after the rains (Oct-Jan) for the most impressive water volume.
