About the Destination

Haputale is a serene hill town perched along the southern edge of Sri Lanka's central highlands, offering breathtaking panoramic views that stretch across mountains, valleys, and distant plains.

Surrounded by lush tea plantations and cool mountain air, Haputale is home to iconic sites like Lipton's Seat and Adisham Bungalow. Its peaceful atmosphere and mist-covered landscapes make it a perfect escape for nature lovers and travelers seeking tranquility.

Key Sites

Haputale Highlights

Lipton's Seat

One of the most impressive viewpoints in Sri Lanka, where Thomas Lipton used to sit and admire the view.

Adisham Bungalow

A beautiful Tudor-style country house and Benedictine monastery.

Dambatenne Tea Factory

A historic tea factory built by Sir Thomas Lipton where you can learn the full tea-making process.

Bambarakanda Falls

The tallest waterfall in Sri Lanka, plunging 263m through rugged pine forests.

Beragala Viewpoint

A breathtaking roadside viewpoint on the Colombo-Badulla road, offering sweeping views of lush valleys and distant mountains.
