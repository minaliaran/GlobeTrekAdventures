Lipton's Seat

The famous viewpoint where Sir Thomas Lipton surveyed his tea empire, offering one of the most spectacular panoramas in Sri Lanka.

History in the Mist

The Scenic Throne of Tea

Lipton's Seat is perhaps the most famous viewpoint in Sri Lanka's hill country. Located in the Dambatenne Tea Estate, it is named after Sir Thomas Lipton, the legendary tea mogul who used to sit here to overlook his vast plantation and the stunning southern plains.

On a clear day, the panorama spans across several provinces, offering glimpses of the southern coast and distant lakes. The journey to the top, winding through lush emerald tea gardens and passing by local pickers at work, is as memorable as the view itself. It remains an essential pilgrimage for both tea lovers and lovers of breathtaking landscapes.

Quick Facts

  • Best Time:
    Arrive before 07:00 AM to catch the sunrise and avoid the mist.
  • Access:
    Accessible by tuk-tuk or a scenic 7km hike from Dambatenne Tea Factory.
  • Refreshments:
    Enjoy a cup of Ceylon tea at the small stall at the summit.
