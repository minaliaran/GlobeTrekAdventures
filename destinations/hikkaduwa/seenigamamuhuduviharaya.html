Seenigama Viharaya

Explore the mystical temple dedicated to the God Devol, perched on a small island just off the Hikkaduwa coast.

Faith & Tradition

The Temple of the Sea

Seenigama Muhudu Viharaya is a unique Buddhist temple famous for its legend and its setting. It is one of the few temples in Sri Lanka situated on a separate island. The temple is dedicated to Devol Deviyo, a local guardian deity believed to have arrived in Sri Lanka from South India and landed at Seenigama.

The temple is a major focal point for devotees who believe in its powerful spiritual energy, particularly for seeking justice or overcoming obstacles. While the mainland part of the temple is always accessible, the island sanctuary is reachable only by a short boat trip, providing a serene and spiritual experience as you leave the mainland behind.

Visitor Protocol

  • Boat Access:
    Small boats are available to take you to the island for a small donation.
  • Dress Code:
    White modest clothing is preferred. Standard sacred site rules apply.
  • Offerings:
    You will see many devotees performing 'poojas' or ritual offerings to God Devol.
  • Festival:
    The annual festival in August is a spectacle of traditional drums and dancing.
