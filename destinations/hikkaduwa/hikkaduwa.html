About the Coastal Town

Hikkaduwa is a vibrant coastal town known for its stunning coral sanctuary and pristine beaches. Whether you're an avid surfer, a diving enthusiast, or simply looking to relax by the ocean, Hikkaduwa has something for everyone.

The town comes alive with an eclectic mix of beachside cafes, seafood restaurants, and lively nightlife, offering travelers an unforgettable mix of relaxation and adventure.

Key Sites

Hikkaduwa Highlights

Hikkaduwa Beach

Golden sands and prime surf spots make it a favorite destination for travelers.

Coral Sanctuary

Snorkel alongside vibrant marine life and sea turtles right off the coast.

Seenigama Temple

An iconic Buddhist temple situated on its own tiny island off the coast.

Tsunami Honganji Viharaya

A peaceful coastal memorial temple dedicated to the victims of the 2004 Indian Ocean Tsunami, offering silence, remembrance, and healing.
