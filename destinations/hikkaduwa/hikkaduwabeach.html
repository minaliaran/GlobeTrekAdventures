Beach & Surf

The Vibrant Coast

Hikkaduwa Beach is one of the most famous and established tourist destinations in Sri Lanka. It was the first beach to attract international surfers in the 1970s and has since evolved into a lively hub of tourism, known for its consistent waves and energetic nightlife.

The beach is divided into several areas, each offering a different experience. From the quiet northern ends perfect for a morning walk to the bustling central area lined with seafood restaurants and surf schools, Hikkaduwa has something for every traveler. It is also one of the few places where you can encounter giant sea turtles right at the water's edge.

Beach Logistics

  • Best Season:
    November to April for the best surf conditions and sunny weather.
  • Surf Point:
    The main break is at the southern end, suitable for intermediate levels.
  • Turtle Spot:
    Giant green turtles often visit the shore near the Hikkaduwa Coral Gardens Hotel.
  • Dining:
    Dozens of beachside restaurants serve fresh seafood catch of the day.
