Tsunami Honganji Viharaya

A peaceful coastal memorial temple dedicated to the victims of the 2004 Indian Ocean Tsunami, offering silence, remembrance, and healing.

Memorial Temple

A Place of Remembrance & Peace

The Tsunami Honganji Viharaya, located near Hikkaduwa, is a solemn memorial site built to honor the lives lost during the devastating 2004 Indian Ocean Tsunami. It stands as a spiritual space where visitors can reflect, pray, and pay respect to those affected by the tragedy.

The temple combines traditional Buddhist architecture with memorial elements, creating a calm atmosphere surrounded by coastal beauty. Many visitors come here not only for religious purposes but also to understand the scale of the disaster and the resilience of local communities who rebuilt their lives.
