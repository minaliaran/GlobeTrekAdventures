About the City

Ratnapura, meaning "City of Gems," is renowned for its dazzling gem mines and lush green landscapes. Nestled in the Sabaragamuwa Province, it has been the hub of Sri Lanka's gem industry for centuries.

Beyond gemstones, Ratnapura offers breathtaking waterfalls, rainforest treks, and rich cultural heritage. Visitors can explore the Batapola Falls, hike through Sinharaja Forest fringes, or witness local gem-cutting workshops, immersing themselves in the vibrant life of this historic city.
