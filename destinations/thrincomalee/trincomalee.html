About the City

Trincomalee is one of Sri Lanka's most captivating coastal cities, known for its deep natural harbor, dramatic headlands, and some of the island's clearest blue water. It feels both sacred and laid-back, with temple bells, sea breezes, and soft white sand shaping the rhythm of the city.

From the cliffside Koneswaram Temple to the coral gardens of Pigeon Island, Trincomalee blends spiritual heritage, colonial history, and marine adventure into one unforgettable east coast destination.

Key Sites

Must-Visit Attractions

Nilaveli & Uppuveli

Stunning white sands and calm blue waters, perfect for relaxation and sunbathing.

Koneswaram Temple

An ancient Hindu temple with breathtaking views from its cliffside position.

Fort Frederick

A historic Portuguese fort with grazing deer and incredible coastal fortifications.

Pigeon Island National Park

A national park and world-class snorkeling spot home to coral reefs and sharks.

Marble Beach

An emerald-water cove surrounded by jungle, offering a serene swimming experience.
