Beach & Nature

The Hidden Cove

Marble Beach, located in Trincomalee within the Air Force base grounds, is often cited as the most beautiful beach on the island. It's situated in a protected bay that keeps the water incredibly calm, resembling a natural swimming pool. The name comes from the surface of the sea, which on a clear day looks as smooth and polished as marble.

Unlike the long stretches of Nilaveli, Marble Beach is a smaller cove fringed by jungle-covered hills. This provides a sense of seclusion and natural beauty that is rare to find. It is particularly popular for swimming, as there are almost no currents, and the water stays crystal clear throughout the day. It's a true hidden gem for those seeking a tranquil and high-quality beach experience.

Coastal Quick Facts

  • Management:
    Managed by the Sri Lanka Air Force. The beach is very well maintained and safe.
  • Swimming:
    The safest and calmest beach for swimming in Trincomalee. Excellent for families.
  • Facilities:
    A small Air Force-run restaurant provides snacks, lunch, and refreshingly cold drinks.
  • Location:
    About 15km south of Trincomalee town Center. A tuk-tuk is the best way to get there.
