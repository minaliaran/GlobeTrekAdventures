Marine National Park

Pigeon Island

Experience world-class snorkeling in one of the most vibrant coral reef systems in the Indian Ocean, home to turtles and Blacktip Reef Sharks.

Marine Wildlife

The Living Reef

Pigeon Island is one of Sri Lanka's two marine national parks. Located just 1km off the coast of Nilaveli, it consists of two islands (Large and Small Pigeon Island) surrounded by exceptional coral reefs. Large Pigeon Island is fringed by a coral reef about 200m long and 100m wide, which sustains over 100 species of corals and 300 species of reef fish.

It is the premier snorkeling and diving destination in Sri Lanka. The shallow waters allow even beginner snorkelers to encounter Blacktip Reef Sharks (which are harmless to humans) and Hawksbill Sea Turtles in their natural environment. The crystal clear visibility and stunning biodiversity make it an essential visit for any ocean lover.

Explorer Protocol

  • Park Fees:
    Foreigners must pay a national park entrance fee at the departmental office on the mainland before boarding a boat.
  • Boat Ride:
    Standard boat rides from Nilaveli Beach take approx. 15-20 minutes. Keep your life jacket on.
  • Conservation:
    Strictly NO touching of the coral or the sharks. Do not collect shells or coral pieces.
  • Snorkel Gear:
    Can be rented from the numerous dive shops at either Nilaveli or Uppuveli Beach.
