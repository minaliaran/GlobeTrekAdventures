The Legend of Ceylon

The Green Gold

Nuwara Eliya is the capital of Sri Lanka's tea industry. The cool climate, high altitude, and mist-covered hills provide the perfect environment for the growth of 'High Grown' Ceylon tea, world-renowned for its distinct flavor and golden color.

A visit to a tea factory is an essential experience. You can walk through the plantations, see the skilled tea pluckers at work, and learn about the entire process from withering and rolling to fermentation and grading. The tour ends with a tasting of the freshest tea you have ever encountered.
