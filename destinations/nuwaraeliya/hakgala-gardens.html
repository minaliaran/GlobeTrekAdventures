Hakgala Botanical Garden

Explore the high-altitude flora of Sri Lanka's second largest botanical gardens, nestled in the shadow of the Hakgala Rock.

Flora & Legend

The Rock Garden

Established in 1861, Hakgala Botanical Garden is situated about 10km from Nuwara Eliya. It is perched on a hill above the Seetha Eliya plateau and was originally used for the cultivation of Cinchona (for quinine).

The garden is most famous for its stunning collection of roses, orchids, and ferns that thrive in the cool mountain air. According to the Hindu epic Ramayana, this area is believed to be part of Ashok Vatika, the site where the demon king Ravana held the goddess Seetha captive.

Explorer Tips

  • Best Time:
    The peak flowering season is from March to April. Arrive in the morning to avoid fog.
  • Key Highlight:
    The Rose Garden and the beautiful Fernery are must-visits.
  • Entry Fee:
    Approx. 1,500 LKR (Foreign adults).
  • Weather Prep:
    Rain is common here. Always carry an umbrella or a rain poncho.
