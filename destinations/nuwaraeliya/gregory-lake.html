Highland Reservoir

Gregory Lake

The historic heart of Nuwara Eliya, offering a perfect blend of recreational activities and scenic highland beauty.

Recreation & Scenery

The Cool Escape

Constructed in 1873 by Governor Sir William Gregory, Gregory Lake is a landmark of the British era in Nuwara Eliya. Originally intended for electricity generation, it quickly became a center for leisure and water sports.

Today, the lake is surrounded by beautifully landscaped gardens and walkways. Visitors can enjoy swan-shaped paddle boats, speed boat rides, and horse riding along the banks. It's a popular spot for families to enjoy the crisp highland air and the stunning views of the surrounding mountains.

Visitor Information

  • Best Time:
    Late morning or early afternoon for clear skies. Sunset can be chilly!
  • Activities:
    Swan boats, jet skis, and speed boat tours are available at the main jetty.
  • Entry Fee:
    Approx. 200 LKR for the lake park area entrance.
  • Weather Tip:
    Temperature drops quickly after 4 PM. Bring a light jacket or sweater.
