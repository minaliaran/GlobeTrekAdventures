Highland National Park

Horton Plains

Hike through the mist-covered plateau to reach the spectacular 880m sheer drop of World's End.

Adventure & Nature

The Edge of the Earth

Horton Plains National Park is a protected area in the central highlands of Sri Lanka. Situated at an altitude of 2,100-2,300 meters, it is covered by montane grassland and cloud forest. The park is most famous for World's End, a sheer precipice with a drop of about 880m.

The 9km circular trail takes you through Baker's Falls and Chimney Pool, offering sightings of endemic wildlife like the Sambar deer and various bird species. The trail ends with the breathtaking views of the southern plains from the precipice.

Trail Logistics

  • Best Time:
    Depart by 06:00 AM. Mist often covers the view after 10 AM.
  • Trail Info:
    Circular route of 9.5km. Takes approximately 3 to 4 hours. Moderate difficulty.
  • Plastic Ban:
    Very strict. No plastic bags or bottles are allowed inside the park.
  • Dress Code:
    Wear layers. It can be freezing in the morning and warm by midday.
