About the City

Nuwara Eliya is a charming hill station nestled in Sri Lanka's central highlands, known for its cool climate, lush tea plantations, and colonial-era architecture that earned it the nickname "Little England."

Surrounded by misty mountains and scenic landscapes, the town offers attractions such as Gregory Lake, Hakgala Botanical Gardens, and endless rolling tea estates, making it a peaceful retreat for travelers seeking nature and relaxation.
