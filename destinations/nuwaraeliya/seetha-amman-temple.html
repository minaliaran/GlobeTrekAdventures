Epic Mythology Site

Seetha Amman Temple

The sacred mythological site where Princess Seetha was believed to be held captive, nestled in a peaceful highland forest.

Mythology & Peace

The Ashok Vatika

Located in the village of Seetha Eliya, just a few kilometers from Nuwara Eliya, the Seetha Amman Temple is unique as it is dedicated to the goddess Seetha. History and legend suggest this is the place where King Ravana held Seetha captive after her abduction.

The temple's colorful Dravidian architecture, the flow of the neighboring stream, and the giant 'footprints' in the rocks (believed to be of Lord Hanuman) attract many Hindu devotees and tourists. It's a spiritually uplifting spot surrounded by the tranquility of the central highlands.

    The footprints of Lord Hanuman in the rocks near the river.

    Modest clothing is required to show respect to the temple.
  • The Stream:
    The river is believed to have originated from the tears of Seetha.
