Galle Fort

Explore the living history of the best-preserved colonial sea fortress in Asia, a vibrant blend of history and culture.

History & Culture

The Old Town

Built by the Portuguese in 1588 and extensively fortified by the Dutch during the 17th century, the Galle Fort is a UNESCO World Heritage site. Unlike many other forts, this is a 'living' fort, housing a multi-ethnic and multi-religious community within its massive ramparts.

Wander through cobblestone streets lined with boutique shops, art galleries, and colonial villas. The fort remains the beating heart of the southern coast, where centuries-old history meets modern Sri Lankan life.

Explore The Fort

  • Best Way to Explore:
    By foot. All major sites are within easy walking distance inside the walls.
  • Best Time:
    Early morning or late afternoon (after 04:00 PM) to avoid the intense sun.
  • Entry Fee:
    Free to enter the fort. Some museums inside have separate fees.
  • Photo Spot:
    The Pedlar Street and the Lighthouse during the golden hour.
