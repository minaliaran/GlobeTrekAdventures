Architecture & History

The Great Church

The Dutch Reformed Church in Galle is one of the oldest Protestant churches still in use in Sri Lanka. It was built on the highest point inside the Fort by the Dutch to express their gratitude for the birth of a daughter to the then Governor Casparus de Jong.

The church's interior is notable for its simplicity and the collection of intricately carved tombstones from the floor, bearing the names of Dutch officials. The structure itself, with its massive gables and walls, remains an architectural landmark of the southern capital.

Visitor Information

    09:00 AM - 05:00 PM. Best visited when sunlight hits the stained glass.

    Donations are welcome. No fixed ticket price.

    Keep voice levels low as this is still an active place of worship.
