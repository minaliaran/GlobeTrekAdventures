Scenic Coastal Walk

Lighthouse & Ramparts

The iconic white lighthouse and the massive granite ramparts that protect the fort from the Indian Ocean waves.

Landmarks

The Beacon of the South

The Galle Lighthouse is the oldest light station in Sri Lanka, dating back to 1848. The current structure, standing at 26.5 meters tall, was built in 1939. It sits on the Point Utrecht Bastion, providing a perfect vantage point for early seafarers.

Walking the ramparts is a beloved pastime for both locals and travelers. The circuit takes you around the entire fort, offering stunning views of the ocean on one side and the red-tiled roofs of the old town on the other.

Explorer Tips

  • Sunset Ritual:
    Join the locals on the ramparts for the best sunset view in the southern coast.
  • Rampart Walk:
    A full loop takes about 60-90 minutes at a leisurely pace.
  • Caution:
    Extremely limited shade. Bring water and wear sun protection.
  • The Shot:
    The view of the lighthouse through the palm trees is the quintessential Galle photo.
