About Galle

Galle is a living monument to the island's colonial past. A blend of Dutch, Portuguese, and British influences, the historic Galle Fort is a UNESCO World Heritage site that has survived centuries of change.

Today, it is a vibrant cultural hub where old villas have been transformed into boutique hotels, art galleries, and eclectic cafes, all guarded by massive stone ramparts that echo the sound of the Indian Ocean.
