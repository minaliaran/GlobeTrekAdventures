About the Landmark

Sigiriya, also known as the Lion Rock, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of Sri Lanka's most iconic landmarks. Rising dramatically from the central plains, this ancient fortress city was built by King Kashyapa in the 5th century AD.

Visitors can climb the rock to explore the breathtaking frescoes, mirror wall inscriptions, and the remnants of royal gardens. The panoramic views from the summit showcase the surrounding jungles and villages, offering a perfect blend of history, culture, and natural beauty.

Sophisticated hydraulic systems that still function after 1,500 years.

Witness the 'Great Gathering' of hundreds of wild elephants nearby.
