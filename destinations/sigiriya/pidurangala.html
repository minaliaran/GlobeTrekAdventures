Pidurangala Rock

The ultimate platform for panoramic views of Sigiriya, offering a spiritual and challenging hiking experience.

Adventure & Spirit

The Sacred Lookout

Pidurangala Rock is a massive rock formation situated a few kilometers north of Sigiriya. It has a long history, believed to be the site of a monastery associated with the royal family during King Kashyapa's reign. The climb up the ancient stone stairs passes by a massive reclining Buddha statue and historic caves.

The climb to the summit is a popular alternative or addition to Sigiriya. The hike's final segment involves some light rock scrambling, but the reward is a spectacular, unobstructed view of the Lion Rock itself, especially stunning at sunrise or sunset. It offers a more rugged, raw connection to the surrounding wilderness compared to the built-up fortress next door.

Visitor Tips

  • Best Time:
    For the most iconic photos of Sigiriya, arrive for sunrise (around 05:30 AM).
  • Difficulty:
    Moderate to difficult toward the end. Wear good hiking shoes.
  • Entry Fee:
    Around 1,000 LKR. Cheaper than Sigiriya!
  • Dress Code:
    Shoulders and knees must be covered to enter the monastery area at the base.
