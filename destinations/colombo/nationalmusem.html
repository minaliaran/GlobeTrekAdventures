Historical Legacy

The Story of Sri Lanka

Established in 1877 by Sir William Henry Gregory, the National Museum is the largest and oldest museum in Sri Lanka. The stunning white architecture itself is a masterpiece, surrounded by the lush gardens of Cinnamon Gardens.

Inside, the museum houses the throne and crown of the Kandyan monarchs, alongside ancient stone carvings, palm-leaf manuscripts, and religious artifacts that span over 2,000 years of the island's rich civilization.
