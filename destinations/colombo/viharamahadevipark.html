Urban Oasis

Viharamahadevi Park

The oldest and largest park in Colombo, offering a lush escape with flowering trees, water fountains, and a golden Buddha statue.

Nature in the City

The Green Heart of Colombo

Originally known as Victoria Park, it was renamed in honor of Queen Viharamahadevi, the mother of King Dutugemunu. The park is famous for its vibrant flowering trees, especially during the spring season, and its large central fountain which comes alive at night.

Perfect for family picnics, morning jogs, or simply a quiet moment in nature, the park also sits adjacent to the Colombo Town Hall, another architectural masterpiece reminiscent of the US Capitol building.
