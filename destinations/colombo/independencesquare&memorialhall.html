A Path to Freedom

The Foundation of Modern Sri Lanka

Independence Memorial Hall is a grand monument built to commemorate the independence of Sri Lanka from British rule on February 4th, 1948. The architecture is inspired by the "Magul Maduwa" (Kandyan Audience Hall) of the 14th century, featuring stone carvings that tell stories of the island's kings.

Today, the square is a favorite spot for locals and tourists alike for jogging, evening strolls, and relaxing in the shade of the grand stone pillars. It stands as a powerful symbol of unity and sovereignty in the heart of Cinnamon Gardens.

Visitor Information

  • Opening Hours:
    Open 24 Hours. Ideally visited early morning or late evening.
  • Photography:
    Excellent for architectural photography. Commercial shoots require a permit.
  • Entry Fee:
    Free Entry to the square and main hall.
  • Inside Tip:
    Visit the Independence Memorial Museum located below the hall to see the artifacts of national heroes.
