About the City

Anuradhapura is one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world and the first capital of ancient Sri Lanka. It was the center of Theravada Buddhism for more than a thousand years.

The city is famous for its massive stupas, ancient monasteries, and sacred sites that still attract pilgrims from around the world today.
