The Sacred Bodhi Tree

Sri Maha Bodhi

The oldest human-planted tree in the world with a known planting date, a sacred sapling from the original Bodhi Tree in India.

Spiritual Heritage

The Living Legend

The Jaya Sri Maha Bodhi is a sacred fig tree in the Mahamewna Gardens. It is believed to be a sapling from the historical Bodhi Tree under which Siddhartha Gautama attained Enlightenment. It was brought to Sri Lanka by Sangamitta Theri, the daughter of Emperor Ashoka, in the 3rd century BC.

For over 2,300 years, the tree has been cared for by successive kings and devotees, making it a powerful symbol of the long-standing Buddhist tradition in Sri Lanka. It remains one of the most sacred sites for Buddhists worldwide.
