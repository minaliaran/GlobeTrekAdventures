About the Destination

Arugam Bay is a small crescent-shaped bay on the southeastern coast of Sri Lanka. Long considered one of the top ten surf spots in the world, its famous 'Main Point' break attracts surfers from around the globe between April and October.

Beyond the waves, Arugam Bay offers a unique blend of nature and culture - from crocodile-filled lagoons and elephant-rich national parks to ancient temples rising from the dunes. Its relaxed, bohemian atmosphere makes it one of Sri Lanka's most loved coastal escapes.

Key Sites

Arugam Bay Highlights

Main Point

The iconic right-hand point break that put Arugam Bay on the world surf map.

Pottuvil Lagoon

A serene lagoon teeming with crocodiles, water birds, and stunning sunset views.

Kumana National Park

A wildlife sanctuary famous for its vast bird colonies and elephant herds.

Muhudu Maha Viharaya

An ancient coastal temple with a romantic legend, set dramatically by the sea.

Panama Tank

A vast ancient reservoir harboring an incredible biodiversity and cultural heritage.
