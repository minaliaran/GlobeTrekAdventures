Ancient Heritage

Muhudu Maha Viharaya

An ancient seaside temple ruins where history meets the shifting sands of the East Coast beach.

Historical Legacy

The Temple in the Dunes

Built over 2,000 years ago by King Kavan Tissa, Muhudu Maha Viharaya was once a grand royal temple complex. It is legendary for being the spot where Princess Viharamahadevi - the future mother of King Dutugemunu - landed when her father, the King of Kelaniya, cast her out into the sea to appease the gods.

Today, the temple stands as a collection of stunning stone statues and ruins, partially buried by the shifting coastal sand dunes. The serene atmosphere of the ancient site, combined with the sound of the Indian Ocean, makes it one of the most spiritual experiences in Pottuvil.

Visitor Information

  • Opening Hours:
    Open 06:00 AM - 07:00 PM. No entry fee required.
  • Dress Code:
    Temple etiquette applies (shoulders and knees covered). Remove shoes before entering the temple area.
  • Location:
    Pottuvil, on the coastal dunes. 10-15 mins from Arugam Bay Main Point.
  • Pro Tip:
    Visit at sunset for spectacular lighting over the ancient statues and the ocean.
