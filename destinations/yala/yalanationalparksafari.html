Wildlife & Adventure

The Wild Frontier

Yala National Park is the second largest and most visited national park in Sri Lanka. It is divided into five blocks, two of which are currently open to the public. The park is world-renowned for its variety of wild animals, but its most famous inhabitant is the Sri Lankan Leopard. Block I specifically has the highest density of leopards in the world, making it the best place for sightings.

Beyond leopards, Yala is home to significant populations of Asian elephants, sloth bears, water buffalo, and crocodiles. The landscape is a diverse mix of semi-arid thorn scrub, lagoons, and golden beaches along the Indian Ocean. A safari in Yala is not just a search for animals; it's an immersion into one of Asia's most balanced and beautiful ecosystems.

Safari Logistics

  • Best Time:
    February to June during the dry season, when animals gather around water holes. Early morning (06:00 AM) or late afternoon (03:00 PM) are best.
  • Park Closure:
    Block I usually closes for maintenance and conservation during September and October. Check before booking.
  • Entry Fees:
    Tickets must be purchased at the entrance office. Standard fees apply for foreign adults and children.
  • Ethics:
    Do not feed animals, stay inside the jeep, and keep noise to a minimum to respect the wildlife.
