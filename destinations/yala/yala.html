About the Park

Yala National Park is Sri Lanka’s most iconic wildlife destination, where vast grasslands, dense forests, and coastal lagoons create a truly untamed landscape. It is famous for having one of the highest densities of leopards in the world, along with elephants, sloth bears, and countless bird species.

From thrilling safari drives at sunrise to ancient ruins hidden deep within the jungle, Yala offers a raw and unforgettable connection to nature. Here, every moment feels alive — where the wilderness meets the ocean in perfect harmony.
