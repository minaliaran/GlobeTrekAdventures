Ravana Falls

Discover one of Sri Lanka's widest waterfalls, steeped in ancient legend and cascading gracefully through the Ella gap.

Nature & Folklore

The Mythical Waters

Ravana Falls is one of the most popular attractions in the country. Standing at approximately 25 meters (82 feet) high, it is a wide-layered waterfall that is particularly stunning during the rainy season. The falls are named after the legendary King Ravana, who according to the Hindu epic Ramayana, is said to have hidden Princess Sita in the caves behind the falling water.

Located right off the main road connecting Ella to the south, the falls are easily accessible. They provide a refreshing stop for travelers. While the main pool at the base is often busy, the sheer power and scale of the cascades against the lush green cliffside remain a truly majestic sight in the heart of the central highlands.

Explore The Falls

  • Accessibility:
    The falls are right by the Ella-Wellawaya road. No trekking is required for the main view.
  • Safety Warning:
    Do not climb the rocks. They are incredibly slippery and accidents are frequent. Keep to designated viewing areas.
  • Best Season:
    The Rainy Season (Nov - Jan) offers the most dramatic, full-flow cascades.
  • Photo Tip:
    Best light is in the early morning; the afternoon sun can create harsh shadows on the falls.
