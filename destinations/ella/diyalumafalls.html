Majestic Waterfall

Diyaluma Falls

Sri Lanka's second highest waterfall, cascading down 220 meters amidst lush highlands.

Nature & Adventure

The Highland Cascade

Diyaluma Falls is located near Koslanda, in the Badulla District, and is one of the tallest waterfalls in Sri Lanka. The waterfall descends in multiple tiers over rocky cliffs into the lush valleys below, creating stunning scenery for visitors and photographers alike.

Adventurous travelers can hike to the top of the falls for panoramic views and even enjoy a natural infinity pool. Early morning visits are perfect to see the mist rising from the falls and to avoid crowds.

Traveler Tips

  • Hike to Top:
    The trail to the top takes 30-45 minutes and offers spectacular views.
  • Best Time:
    Morning is ideal for misty photography and cooler temperatures.
  • Transport:
    Accessible by car from Ella or Koslanda; parking is limited near the trailhead.
  • Safety:
    Wear sturdy shoes for the hike and avoid slippery rocks near the edge.
