Architecture & History

The Bridge in the Sky

The Nine Arch Bridge in Demodara, between Ella and Demodara railway stations, is one of the best examples of colonial-era railway construction in the world. Built in 1921, the bridge stands 80 feet high and spans 300 feet. It is famous for being constructed without any metal support; due to the steel shortage during WWI, it was built entirely of bricks, cement, and solid rock.

Walking along the tracks or standing in the lush tea fields below, visitors can witness the majestic curve of the bridge as the iconic blue or red trains pass over. It is a stunning blend of human ingenuity and tropical nature, becoming a symbol of Sri Lankan travel and one of the most photographed locations in the country.

Explore Local Knowledge

  • Best Time:
    Check the train timetable at Ella station. There are usually 5-6 passenger trains passing per day. Arrive 20 mins early.
  • Access:
    Walk along the tracks from Ella town (approx. 25-30 mins) or take a tuk-tuk to the jungle path entrance.
  • Best View:
    Small cafes in the surrounding hills offer panoramic views of the bridge while you wait for the train.
  • Important:
    Always listen for the train horn and keep a safe distance from the tracks when the train arrives.
