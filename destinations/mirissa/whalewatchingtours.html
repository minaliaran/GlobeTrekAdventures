The Deep Blue Adventure

Mirissa is the premier spot in Sri Lanka for whale watching. The continental shelf is at its narrowest here, which means deeper waters are closer to the shore, attracting large numbers of marine mammals. Blue Whales are most commonly seen during the season.

Tours depart early in the morning from the Mirissa harbor. Being on the open water as the sun rises, accompanied by pods of Spinner Dolphins, is an experience you will never forget. It is one of the most sustainable ways to witness these majestic creatures in their natural habitat.

Tour Logistics

  • Duration:
    Typically 3-5 hours. Best viewing is between 7:00 AM and 11:00 AM.
  • Season:
    Best sightings from November to April when the seas are calm.
  • Safety:
    Certified boats only. Life jackets are provided and mandatory.
  • Photography:
    Bring a zoom lens and protection for your gear from sea spray.
