Beach Sanctuary

The Tropical Hideaway

True to its name, Secret Beach is a hidden gem tucked away around the corner from the main Mirissa coastline. It consists of three small, interconnected coves: Blue Lagoon, Palm Cove, and Shelley's Beach.

With its shallow, protected pools, it is perfect for swimming and snorkeling. The beach is a favorite among those looking for a more intimate and less commercialized tropical experience. Access involves a short trek or a Tuktuk ride, making the arrival feel like a true discovery.

Visitor Tips

  • Access:
    A 20-min hilly hike from central Mirissa or a steep Tuktuk ride.
  • Snorkeling:
    The Blue Lagoon cove is generally calm and great for spotting tropical fish.
  • Facilities:
    A small beach bar offers food and cold drinks in a very rustic setting.
  • Responsible Travel:
    Please respect the natural beauty; take your litter back with you.
