Parrot Rock

The small, iconic island formation at the edge of Mirissa beach, offering the best aerial perspective of the bay.

Beach Lookout

The Island Bridge

Parrot Rock is a small rocky outcrop situated just offshore at the main beach in Mirissa. It is connected to the mainland by a narrow sandbar that is often submerged during high tide. It is one of the most recognizable points on the Mirissa coastline.

Visitors can climb the set of rustic stairs to reach the top, where they are rewarded with a stunning view across Mirissa Bay and the surrounding reef. It's a popular spot for photography and for watching the surfers catch waves just beyond the rock.

Lookout Information

  • Tide Check:
    Check the tide times. The path is easiest to cross during low tide.
  • Footwear:
    The climb can be rocky. Wear flip-flops or water shoes for the crossing.
  • Duration:
    A visit takes about 20-30 minutes. Perfect for a quick detour.
  • Viewpoint:
    The best spot for seeing the entire horseshoe shape of the beach.
