Architecture & Relics

The Relic Sanctuary

The Polonnaruwa Vatadage is situated in the Dalada Maluwa (Quadrangle) area and was built to protect the sacred tooth relic of the Buddha. The current structure is attributed to King Parakramabahu I in the 12th century, though it may have earlier origins.

The circular structure is a masterpiece of stone architecture, with four entrances facing the cardinal directions. Each entrance is flanked by two elaborate guard stones and fronted by a pristine 'Sandakada Pahana' (Moonstone), widely considered to be the most beautiful of their kind.

Site Highlights

  • Moonstone:
    Observe the intricate details of the moonstone at the northern entrance, showing the cycle of life.
  • Guard Stones:
    The Nagara-style guard stones are some of the best preserved in the country.
  • Protocol:
    Standard sacred site dress (white/modest) is mandatory. Shoes to be removed.
  • Best Shot:
    The view through the entrance stairs towards the seated Buddha statue.
