The Largest Stupa

Rankoth Vehera

The tallest and most majestic stupa in Polonnaruwa, built to match the ancient scale of the Anuradhapura kingdoms.

Architecture & Scale

The Golden Pinnacle

Built by King Nissanka Malla in the 12th century, Rankoth Vehera (The Golden Pinnacle) is the largest stupa in Polonnaruwa with a diameter of about 170 feet and a height of 177 feet. It was explicitly designed to mimic the majestic Ruwanwelisaya of Anuradhapura.

Constructed entirely of bricks, the stupa is part of the Alahana Pirivena monastic complex. Its massive size and the remains of the Vahalkada (frontispieces) at the cardinal directions represent the final grand architectural flourish of the medieval kingdom before the capital moved south.

Explorer Information

  • Significance:
    Notice the stone inscriptions left by King Nissanka Malla detailing the building's history.
  • Time:
    Best visited in the morning (08:00 AM) to comfortably walk the circle before the bricks heat up.
  • Dress Code:
    White modest clothing required as this is an active sacred site.
  • Caution:
    Footwear is removed far from the base. The sand around the stupa can get very hot after noon.
