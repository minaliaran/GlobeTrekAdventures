Bar Reef Sanctuary

The largest and most pristine coral reef system in Sri Lanka, home to incredible marine biodiversity and crystal-clear waters.

Underwater Discovery

The Vibrant Ocean Garden

The Bar Reef is an extensively developed and highly diverse coral reef system located off the coast of the Kalpitiya peninsula. Covering approximately 300 square kilometers, it is a protected marine sanctuary and one of the best locations for snorkeling and diving in Sri Lanka.

The reef is home to over 150 species of hard coral and a vast array of marine life, including manta rays, sea turtles, and hundreds of species of colorful reef fish. Exploring the Bar Reef provides a firsthand view of a thriving underwater ecosystem, where the sunlight filters through clear tropical waters to reveal the stunningly intricate beauty of the largest coral garden in the island.

Quick Facts

  • Snorkeling:
    Best done between November and April when the sea is calm and visibility is maximum.
  • Conservation:
    As a protected area, do not touch or step on any coral. "Take only memories, leave only bubbles."
  • Access:
    Reached by a 45-minute to 1-hour boat ride from the Kalpitiya mainland.
