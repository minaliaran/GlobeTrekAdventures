The Deep Blue Wonders

Kalpitiya is one of the best locations in Sri Lanka to see dolphins and whales. For dolphins, it's particularly famous for the massive pods of Spinner Dolphins that frequent the coastal waters, often numbering in the hundreds and putting on a spectacular show of jumps and spins.

Further out, the deeper seas around the peninsula are a seasonal habitat for Sperm Whales and the occasional Blue Whale. Heading out into the ocean during the calm morning hours offers an unforgettable encounter with these majestic creatures in their natural home, making Kalpitiya a must-visit for any wildlife enthusiast.

Quick Facts

  • Best Season:
    November to April, when the sea is calmest and visibility is best.
  • Timing:
    Boats typically depart at 07:00 AM as animals are most active in the early morning.
  • What to See:
    Spinner Dolphins are very common; Sperm Whales are the primary whale species spotted here.
