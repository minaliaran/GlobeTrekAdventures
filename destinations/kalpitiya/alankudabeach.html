Alankuda Beach

A peaceful, untouched golden beach in Kalpitiya known for sunsets, calm waters, and eco-resort stays.

Beach Escape

The Hidden Paradise of Kalpitiya

Alankuda Beach is one of the most beautiful and quiet beaches in the Kalpitiya region. It is famous for its long sandy shoreline, peaceful environment, and breathtaking sunsets.

Unlike crowded tourist beaches, Alankuda offers a pure natural experience where visitors can relax, enjoy eco-resorts, and experience authentic coastal village life.
