About the Peninsula

Located in the Puttalam district of Sri Lanka, the Kalpitiya peninsula consists of 14 untouched islands wrapped around the Kalpitiya lagoon and bordered by the Indian Ocean. It is considered one of the best kite destinations in the world thanks to consistent winds.

But the thrills aren't just above the surface - Kalpitiya is the ultimate hotspot for observing massive pods of spinner dolphins and migrating whales passing just off the coast via the deep sea underwater canyon.
