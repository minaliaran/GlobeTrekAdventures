About the City

Badulla is one of the oldest towns in Sri Lanka, nestled in a valley surrounded by tea estates and misty mountains. It was once the capital of the historic Uva Kingdom and is an important hub on Sri Lanka's legendary railway line.

Discover sacred sites like Muthiyangana Raja Maha Vihara, experience the beauty of Dunhinda Falls, and explore the engineering wonder of the Bogoda Wooden Bridge, one of the oldest surviving wooden bridges in the country.

Key Sites

Badulla Highlights

Muthiyangana Temple

An ancient Buddhist temple with a history dating back over 2,500 years, one of the Solosmasthana.

Dunhinda Falls

The "Veil of the Mist," one of the most beautiful and iconic waterfalls in Sri Lanka.

Bogoda Wooden Bridge

The oldest wooden bridge in the world, built on the ancient trail without a single nail.

St. Mark's Church

The historic stone Garrison church of Badulla, a striking piece of colonial architecture.

Namunukula Range

The nine-peaked mountain range dominating the Uva skyline, perfect for high-altitude trekking.
