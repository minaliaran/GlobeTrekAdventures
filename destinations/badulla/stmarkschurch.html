St. Mark's Church

The historic Garrison Church of Badulla, a beautiful stone structure built in the 1840s to serve the British military during the colonial era.

Colonial Architecture

The Gentle Heart of the Church

St. Mark's Garrison Church is a striking example of mid-19th-century colonial architecture. Built in 1846 during the British era, it was primarily established for the Garrison officers and soldiers stationed in the Uva province. The church's design features rugged granite walls and elegant stained glass, giving it a timeless and peaceful atmosphere.

The interior of the church is a museum of colonial history, housing numerous marble plaques and monuments dedicated to the British officers who lived and worked in the hill country. Exploring the quiet courtyard of the church, surrounded by the green hills of Badulla, provides a unique insight into the island's complex colonial heritage and its transition into the high-altitude wilderness of the central highlands.
