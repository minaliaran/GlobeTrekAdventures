An Artist's Sanctuary

Brief Garden

Discover the enchanting and mildly eccentric landscaped gardens created by the renowned artist Bevis Bawa, brother of Geoffrey Bawa.

Landscape & Art

The Hidden Eden

Established in 1929 by Bevis Bawa, Brief Garden was his life's work. Unlike the architectural sprawl of his brother Geoffrey's estates, Brief is more intimate, a series of 'garden rooms' carved out of a former rubber plantation. It is a masterpiece of landscape design, featuring narrow pathways, hidden ponds, and eccentric sculptures.

The garden reflects Bevis's artistic and playful personality, incorporating both Western and Eastern influences. The house itself contains a collection of art and memorabilia, offering a glimpse into the sophisticated bohemian lifestyle of one of Sri Lanka's most influential creative minds during the 20th century.
