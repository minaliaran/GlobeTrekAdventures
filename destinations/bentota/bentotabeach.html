Coast & Luxury

The Scenic Shoreline

Bentota Beach is legendary for its wide expanse of golden sand and its unique position on a narrow strip of land between the Indian Ocean and the Bentota River. This geography makes it the premier resort destination in Sri Lanka, offering both high-end luxury and high-adrenaline activities.

The beach is meticulously maintained and features some of the island's most iconic boutique hotels and resorts. Whether you're looking to spend your day under a thatched umbrella with a coconut or tearing across the waves on a jet ski, Bentota provides a sophisticated and diverse beach experience that is hard to match.

Explorer Information

  • Water Sports:
    The river-end of the beach is the best for jet skiing, windsurfing, and banana boat rides.
  • Best Season:
    December to April for calm seas and perfect swimming conditions.
  • Sunset Hub:
    The beach faces due west, making it one of the best spots on the island for sunset views.
  • Safety Info:
    Lifeguards are stationed near the major hotels. Always check the flags before swimming.
