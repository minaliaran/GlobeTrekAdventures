About the Area

Bentota is a coastal town in the Galle District of Sri Lanka's Southern Province, known for its long golden beaches and the scenic Bentota River.

It is a popular destination for water sports such as jet skiing and windsurfing. Visitors can also enjoy river safaris on Madu Ganga and explore the iconic Brief Garden and Lunuganga estate designed by renowned architect Geoffrey Bawa.

Key Sites

Bentota Highlights

Golden Beach

One of the finest wide beaches in Sri Lanka, perfect for luxury, sunsets, and water sports.

Brief Garden

The enchanting landscaped gardens and home created by the artist Bevis Bawa.

Bentota River Safari

A peaceful boat ride through mangrove tunnels, discovering crocodiles and tropical birds.

Kosgoda Turtle Hatchery

Learn about conservation effort and witness the release of baby sea turtles back to the ocean.

Lunuganga Estate

The iconic country home of architect Geoffrey Bawa, a masterpiece of landscape design.
