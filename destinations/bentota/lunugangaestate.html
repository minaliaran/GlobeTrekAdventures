Architectural Masterpiece

Lunuganga Estate

Step into the masterful landscape garden of the world-renowned architect Geoffrey Bawa, an experimental lab for Tropical Modernism.

Architecture & Design

The Living Canvas

Lunuganga was the private estate of Geoffrey Bawa, Sri Lanka's most celebrated architect and the father of Tropical Modernism. Beginning in 1947, he transformed a derelict rubber plantation into a world-class landscape garden, a process that lasted for over 50 years until his death.

The garden was Bawa's personal laboratory, where he refined architectural ideas through carefully framing views, using light and shadow, and blending built structures seamlessly into the natural environment. It is a place of profound beauty and quiet contemplation, offering a unique insight into the mind of a design legend.
