Nature & Wildlife

The Mangrove Odyssey

The Bentota Ganga river safari is a classic South Coast experience. Boarding a small motorboat, you leave the open ocean and enter a different world of calm waters and dense green vegetation. The highlights of the trip are the narrow mangrove tunnels where the overhead canopy almost touches the water.

Along the way, you can spot water monitors, brilliant kingfishers, and occasionally, small crocodiles basking on the banks. The safari also often includes stops at a cinnamon island where you can learn about the traditional spice trade, and a visit to a small riverside temple.

Safari Logistics

  • Duration:
    Typical safaris last about 1.5 to 2 hours. Best taken in the early morning or late afternoon.
  • Spotting:
    Kingfishers are easy to spot. Ask your boatman to look for crocodiles in the deeper mangroves.
  • Safety:
    Life jackets are provided and must be worn at all times during the boat ride.
  • Photo Tip:
    The light filtering through the mangrove tunnels is perfect for atmospheric shots.
