About the National Park

Udawalawe is renowned for its sprawling national park, home to one of the largest elephant populations in Sri Lanka. The park covers over 30,000 hectares of grasslands, reservoirs, and forests, making it a haven for wildlife enthusiasts.

Visitors can enjoy guided safaris to witness elephants, water buffalo, crocodiles, and a variety of bird species in their natural habitat. The Udawalawe Reservoir adds to the scenic beauty, offering stunning landscapes perfect for photography and nature walks.
