Conservation & Wildlife

The Gentle Nursery

The Elephant Transit Home (ETH) in Udawalawe, established in 1995, is a recognized sanctuary that provides care and rehabilitation for abandoned or orphaned elephant calves from all over Sri Lanka. Unlike other elephant attractions, the ETH is strictly dedicated to conservation - there is minimal human contact, and the goal is always to release the elephants back into the wild once they are strong enough.

Visitors can watch the calves being fed milk from a viewing platform at regular intervals. It is a heartwarming experience to see the social dynamics of the group as they rush to the feeding station. The facility provides important education on the challenges facing wild elephants and the efforts being made to ensure their survival in the modern world.

Visitor Intelligence

  • Feeding Times:
    09:00 AM, 12:00 PM, 03:00 PM, and 06:00 PM daily. Arrive 15 minutes early to secure a good spot.
  • Ethical Policy:
    Strictly NO touching, feeding, or close-up interaction. This is crucial for their eventual release.
  • Entry:
    Entrance fee goes directly towards the care and milk for the calves. Tickets at the gate.
  • Photo Tip:
    Bring a zoom lens; the platform is set back to minimize stress for the animals.
