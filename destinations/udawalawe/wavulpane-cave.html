Wavulpane Cave

Discover the ancient limestone caves of Wavulpane, featuring a rare internal waterfall, prehistoric fossils, and a massive ecosystem of bats.

Adventure & Exploration

The Cave of Millions

The Wavulpane Cave, located in the Bulutota Rakwana mountain range near Udawalawe, is an ancient limestone cave estimated to be over 500 million years old. Its name, which means 'Place of Bats', is fitting, as the cave is home to hundreds of thousands of bats. The cave is famous for its unique internal ecosystem and the stunning 100-foot waterfall that plunges through its center.

Exploring the cave is a true adventure. You'll walk through dark, damp passages filled with stalactites and stalagmites, and you might even spot prehistoric fossils embedded in the limestone walls. It's an immersive experience for those who don't mind getting a bit muddy or the occasional smell of bat guano. The sheer scale of the internal waterfall in such a prehistoric setting is a rare natural wonder.

Explorer Intel

  • Footwear:
    Wear sturdy, waterproof shoes with a good grip. The cave floor is very slippery and wet.
  • Lighting:
    A high-quality torch or headlamp is essential. Hiring a local guide with extra lighting is recommended.
  • Clothing:
    Wear clothes you don't mind getting dirty. You will likely get wet from the internal waterfall spray.
  • Best Season:
    Avoid visiting during heavy rains, as the water level inside the cave can rise dangerously fast.
