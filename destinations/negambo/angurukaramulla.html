Epic Buddhist Art

Angurukaramulla Temple

Negombo's most iconic Buddhist landmark, famous for its massive seated Buddha statue and vibrant mural heritage.

Ancient Masterpiece

The Lion's Mouth Entrance

Angurukaramulla Temple is a visual marvel, greeting visitors with its massive entrance shaped like a lion's mouth. This ancient site is home to a magnificent 6-meter-tall seated Buddha statue, surrounded by intricately carved figures and stunning traditional art.

The temple is a living gallery of Buddhist education, featuring a large hall adorned with detailed paintings and sculptures depicting the life of the Buddha and various Buddhist legends. Exploring its spiritual grounds offers a serene and culturally enriching experience, showcasing the deep-rooted Buddhist traditions of the Negombo region.

Visitor Information

  • Etiquette:
    Cover shoulders and knees. Remove hats and footwear before entering.
  • Heritage:
    Check out the remains of a library that was built over 300 years ago.
  • Photography:
    Avoid turning your back to Buddha statues while taking photos.
  • Best Time:
    Early morning (08:00 AM) for peaceful exploration.
