About the City

Negombo is a vibrant coastal city located just north of Colombo, known for its long sandy beaches, bustling fishing industry, and rich colonial heritage influenced by Portuguese and Dutch history.

The city is famous for its scenic lagoon, colorful fish markets, and historic churches. With its relaxed seaside atmosphere and proximity to the international airport, Negombo is often the first stop for travelers arriving in Sri Lanka.
