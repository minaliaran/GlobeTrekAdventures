Hindu Temple

Sri Singama Kali Amman Kovil

A sacred and vibrant Hindu temple in Negombo, revered for its powerful deity and colourful gopuram adorned with intricate traditional sculptures.

Sacred Heritage

A Temple of Devotion & Grace

The Sri Singama Kali Amman Kovil is one of the most prominent Hindu temples in the Negombo region, dedicated to Goddess Kali Amman - a powerful and protective deity worshipped widely by the Tamil Hindu community. The temple is renowned for its magnificent gopuram (tower gateway), lavishly decorated with colourful stucco sculptures of gods, goddesses, and mythological figures.

Visitors are welcome to witness the devout daily rituals, hear the resonant chanting of Vedic hymns, and experience the fragrant offerings of flowers and incense that fill the air. The temple comes alive during major festivals such as Navarathri and Thaipusam, when thousands of devotees gather for processions, classical music, and traditional dance performances - offering a profoundly moving cultural and spiritual experience.

Visitor Information

  • Deity:
    Goddess Kali Amman, worshipped for protection and blessings.
  • Dress Code:
    Modest attire required. Footwear must be removed at the entrance.
  • Hours:
    06:00 AM - 12:00 PM and 04:00 PM - 08:00 PM daily.
  • Festivals:
    Navarathri and Thaipusam are major celebration periods.
  • Photography:
    Permitted in outer areas; seek permission for inner sanctums.
