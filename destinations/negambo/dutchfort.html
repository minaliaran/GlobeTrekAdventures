Colonial History

The Legacy of Negombo Fort

The Dutch Fort in Negombo was originally built by the Portuguese in the 16th century and later rebuilt and strengthened by the Dutch in the 17th century after they took control of the coastal region.

Although much of the original fort has been lost over time, parts of the old walls and gate still remain, reflecting the strategic importance of Negombo during colonial trade routes.

Today, the fort area is located near the Negombo Prison and serves as a historical landmark that attracts visitors interested in Sri Lanka's colonial past.
