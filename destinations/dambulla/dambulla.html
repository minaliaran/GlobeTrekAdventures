About the City

Dambulla is one of Sri Lanka's most significant cultural and religious destinations, located in the central part of the island. It is best known for its ancient cave temple complex and rich Buddhist heritage that dates back over 2,000 years.

The Dambulla Cave Temple (Golden Temple) is a UNESCO World Heritage Site featuring beautifully preserved Buddha statues, murals, and rock-carved shrines. The area is also surrounded by scenic landscapes, spice gardens, and easy access to the Cultural Triangle.
