Ancient Masterpiece

The Spiritual Legacy of the Rock

The Dambulla Cave Temple, also known as the Golden Temple of Dambulla, is a sacred site that has been a center of Buddhist worship for over 22 centuries. Hand-carved into a massive granite outcrop that towers over the surrounding plains, this complex consists of five separate caves, each more breathtaking than the last.

Inside, you'll find a world-renowned gallery of Buddhist art, featuring over 150 stunning statues and nearly 2,100 square meters of intricate murals. These ancient paintings depict various scenes from the Buddha's life and key moments in the history of the island, creating a mystical and deeply meditative atmosphere that has moved visitors for millennia.

Quick Facts

  • Origins:
    Dating back to the 1st century BC, initially established by King Valagamba.
  • Etiquette:
    Shoulders and knees must be covered. Remove hats and shoes before entering the caves.
  • Access:
    A relatively steep climb up stone steps leads to the cave entrance. Expect friendly local monkeys!
