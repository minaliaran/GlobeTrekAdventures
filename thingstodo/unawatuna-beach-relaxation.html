Unawatuna Beach Relaxation

Unawatuna is one of Sri Lanka's most loved beach escapes, known for its golden sand, calm sea views, and easy tropical atmosphere.

About

This page is ideal for travellers who want a simple and relaxing coastal break with beach walks, warm water, and laid-back cafes close to the shore.

What You Can Do

  • Relax on golden sandy beaches.
  • Take peaceful morning and sunset walks by the sea.
  • Swim and enjoy calm beach conditions in season.
  • Visit nearby cafes and small coastal shops.

Tips

  • Visit early morning or sunset for the best atmosphere.
  • Carry sunscreen, sunglasses, and light clothing.
  • Stay near the beach for the easiest access and best views.
  • Keep beachwear and valuables simple for a relaxed trip.

Location

Unawatuna, Southern Coast, Sri Lanka

Best Time to Visit

November to April for the best beach weather.

Price

Budget Hotels: LKR 6,000 - 15,000 per night
