Rainforest Experience

Sinharaja Rainforest Trek

Explore Sri Lanka's best-known rainforest with guided jungle walks, waterfalls, wildlife, and rich tropical scenery.

About

Sinharaja Forest Reserve is Sri Lanka's most famous rainforest destination. It is ideal for travellers who want a guided nature adventure surrounded by dense jungle, streams, birds, and monkeys.

What You Can Do

  • Join a guided rainforest trek through dense jungle paths.
  • See waterfalls, streams, and tropical forest scenery.
  • Watch birds, butterflies, and monkeys in their natural habitat.
  • Experience one of Sri Lanka's top eco-adventures.

Tips

  • Go with a guide because forest routes are not ideal to explore alone.
  • Wear leech socks and shoes with good grip.
  • Carry water, insect repellent, and light rain protection.
  • Choose morning treks for a cooler and more active forest experience.

Location

Sinharaja Forest Reserve, Sri Lanka

Duration

About 3 to 6 hours depending on the guided route.

Cost

Entry: LKR 2,000 - 5,000
Guide: LKR 3,000 - 7,000
Transport from Ratnapura: LKR 2,000 - 6,000

Highlights

Dense jungle, waterfalls, birds, monkeys, and a full rainforest atmosphere.
