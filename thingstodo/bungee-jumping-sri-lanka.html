Bungee Jumping in Sri Lanka

Take on one of the island's most intense adrenaline experiences with a free-fall jump, scenic views, and professional safety support.

About

Bungee jumping in Sri Lanka is a high-adrenaline extreme sport experience designed for thrill seekers who want a true free-fall feeling. It is usually operated as a seasonal or event-based adventure activity with professional safety systems, harnesses, and trained instructors.

What You Can Do

  • Experience a free-fall jump from a high platform.
  • Feel a strong adrenaline rush in just seconds.
  • Capture photos and videos of your jump with optional packages.
  • Receive full safety briefing and training before jumping.
  • Enjoy scenic views from the jump platform.

Tips

  • Go with an empty stomach or only a light meal before jumping.
  • Wear comfortable, secure clothing and closed shoes.
  • Follow all instructor instructions carefully.
  • Remove loose items like phones, hats, and glasses before jumping.
  • Book in advance during tourist seasons.
  • Bring friends for support and photos.
  • Stay calm because the staff will guide you step by step.

Location

Usually available in seasonal adventure setups such as Colombo or Kitulgala event locations, depending on the operator.

Best Time to Visit

Seasonal activity. Best chances are from December to April and July to September depending on event schedules.

Price

Single Jump: LKR 8,000 - 20,000
Photo or Video Package: LKR 2,000 - 5,000
Transport if included: LKR 2,000 - 10,000

Duration

About 1 to 2 hours including briefing, safety checks, and the jump.
