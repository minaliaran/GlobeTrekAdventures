White Water Rafting Kitulgala

Enjoy one of Sri Lanka's best river adventures with exciting rapids, rainforest scenery, and guided rafting suitable for both beginners and active travellers.

About

Kitulgala is Sri Lanka's most popular destination for white water rafting. The Kelani River offers thrilling rapids in a lush jungle setting and is a top pick for outdoor adventure.

What You Can Do

  • Ride exciting river rapids with trained guides.
  • Enjoy teamwork-based adventure with friends or family.
  • Take in rainforest scenery along the river route.
  • Combine rafting with canyoning or confidence jumps nearby.

Tips

  • Wear quick-dry clothes and water-friendly footwear.
  • Follow guide instructions carefully at all times.
  • Leave electronics in waterproof storage unless protected.
  • Bring a towel and change of clothes for after the ride.
  • Book with a trusted operator for safety equipment and trained staff.
