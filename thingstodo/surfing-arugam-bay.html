Surfing in Arugam Bay

Ride one of Sri Lanka's most famous surf destinations, known for its warm water, laid-back vibe, and waves for both beginners and experienced surfers.

About

Arugam Bay is a world-known surfing hotspot on Sri Lanka's east coast. It attracts surfers for its reliable breaks, beach atmosphere, and easy access to surf schools and board rentals.

What You Can Do

  • Surf at Main Point and nearby beach breaks.
  • Take beginner or intermediate surf lessons.
  • Relax at beachfront cafes after sessions.
  • Explore lagoons and nearby coastal scenery.

Tips

  • Visit early for calmer sessions and lighter crowds.
  • Use reef-safe sunscreen and stay hydrated.
  • Choose a surf break that matches your skill level.
  • Rent boards from reliable local surf shops.
  • Watch currents and follow local surf advice.
