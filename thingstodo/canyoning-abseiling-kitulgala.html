Canyoning & Abseiling Kitulgala

Take on waterfalls, rocky descents, and natural pools in one of Sri Lanka's best outdoor adventure environments.

About

Kitulgala is not only famous for rafting but also for canyoning and abseiling. These activities are ideal for travellers who want guided adventure in a rainforest and waterfall setting.

What You Can Do

  • Abseil down waterfalls with proper safety gear.
  • Walk through stream paths and rocky canyon routes.
  • Jump or slide into natural pools in selected areas.
  • Combine the activity with rafting for a full adventure day.

Tips

  • Wear quick-dry clothes and shoes with strong grip.
  • Listen carefully to guide instructions before each descent.
  • Do not carry loose valuables during waterfall activities.
  • Bring an extra set of clothes for after the tour.
  • Choose guided operators with proper helmets and harnesses.
