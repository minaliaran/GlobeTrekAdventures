Cave Experience

Nil Diya Pokuna Blue Cave

Take on an underground adventure with dark cave routes, hidden blue water, ladders, and swimming in one of Sri Lanka's most unusual experiences.

About

Nil Diya Pokuna is an underground cave adventure known for its hidden water pool and challenging cave route. It suits confident travellers who enjoy unusual and physically active experiences.

What You Can Do

  • Explore dark underground cave passages with a guide.
  • See the hidden underground blue water pool.
  • Climb ladders and navigate narrow cave sections.
  • Swim in underground water during the experience.

Tips

  • This activity is not ideal for beginners or claustrophobic visitors.
  • Wear clothes and shoes suitable for water and cave conditions.
  • Follow guide instructions carefully in narrow and dark sections.
  • Carry only essential items because movement inside is limited.
