World's End Hike

Walk one of Sri Lanka's most famous highland trails through cool grasslands, cloud forest, waterfalls, and dramatic cliff views.

About

World's End is one of the most popular hikes in Horton Plains National Park. The trail takes visitors through a cold-climate highland landscape with mist, forest paths, and a famous drop at the cliff viewpoint.

What You Can Do

  • Hike the main 9 km loop through Horton Plains.
  • See the dramatic World's End cliff viewpoint.
  • Visit Baker's Falls along the trail.
  • Experience cloud forest and cool highland scenery.

Tips

  • Go before 9 AM for the clearest view at World's End.
  • Wear comfortable walking shoes and bring a light jacket.
  • Carry water and light snacks for the loop hike.
  • Start early because the weather gets mistier later in the morning.
