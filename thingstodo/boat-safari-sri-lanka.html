Boat Safari in Sri Lanka

Explore Sri Lanka's beautiful rivers and lagoons with a guided boat safari, uncovering wildlife, mangrove forests, and peaceful island scenery.

About

Boat safaris in Sri Lanka take travellers through scenic river paths, mangroves, and coastal lagoons. These tours are excellent for relaxing and seeing wildlife like birds, monitors, and sometimes crocodiles in a tropical setting.

What You Can Do

  • Enjoy a guided boat ride through mangroves and rivers.
  • Watch for local wildlife including birds and water monitors.
  • Visit small islands within rivers, such as Cinnamon Island in Bentota.
  • Experience the peaceful nature of Sri Lanka's inland waters.
  • Learn about local river ecosystems from your boat guide.

Tips

  • The best time for boat safaris is early morning or late afternoon.
  • Wear sunscreen and a hat to protect from the sun.
  • Bring a camera for wildlife and scenery photos.
  • Choose a life jacket and follow all boat safety rules.
  • Ask your guide about specific bird species you see.
  • Bring small cash if you plan to visit cinnamon or temple islands.

Location

Best locations: Bentota (Madu River), Negombo Lagoon, and Kalpitiya.

Best Time to Visit

Year Round. Early mornings (7 AM - 9 AM) and late afternoons (4 PM - 6 PM) are best for wildlife.

Price

Private Boat (1-2 hours): LKR 4,000 - 8,000
Group Tours: LKR 2,000 - 4,000 per person

Duration

1 to 3 hours depending on the river route.
