Hot Air Balloon Sigiriya & Dambulla

See the Cultural Triangle from the sky with sunrise balloon views over Sigiriya Rock, Dambulla, forests, and lakes.

About

This balloon experience focuses on the scenic region around Sigiriya and Dambulla, giving visitors a rare aerial look at one of Sri Lanka's most famous landscapes.

What You Can Do

  • Watch sunrise over the Sigiriya and Dambulla region.
  • Enjoy panoramic aerial views of lakes, forests, and villages.
  • Capture unique photographs of the Cultural Triangle.
  • Combine the flight with a same-day visit to Sigiriya Rock Fortress.

Tips

  • Book ahead during peak season for the best availability.
  • Dress in comfortable layers for an early start.
  • Keep your phone or camera ready for sunrise shots.
  • Confirm the exact launch point with your operator in advance.
  • Expect flight timing to depend on weather and wind conditions.
