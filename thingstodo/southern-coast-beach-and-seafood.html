Coastal Experience

Southern Coast Beach and Seafood

Enjoy the peaceful coastal beauty of Sri Lanka with golden beaches, fresh seafood, and relaxing ocean views along the southern shoreline.

About

This southern coast experience is perfect for travellers who want beach time, delicious seafood, romantic oceanfront moments, and scenic drives between Sri Lanka's most loved coastal towns.

What You Can Do

  • Relax on golden sandy beaches in Unawatuna.
  • Enjoy fresh seafood at beachside restaurants in Hikkaduwa.
  • Experience romantic oceanfront dining in Mirissa.
  • Taste authentic Sri Lankan coastal cuisine.
  • Enjoy scenic ocean views along the southern coastline roads.

Tips

  • Visit early morning or at sunset for the best beach views.
  • Book hotels in advance during peak season from December to March.
  • Try fresh seafood dishes from trusted beachside restaurants.
  • Carry sunscreen, sunglasses, and light clothing.
  • Stay near the beach for the most relaxing experience.
  • Plan short stops between coastal towns for scenic photos.
