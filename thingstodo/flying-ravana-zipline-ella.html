Flying Ravana Zipline

Experience Sri Lanka's first mega zipline in Ella and fly over green tea plantations with amazing mountain views.

About

Flying Ravana is one of Ella's most exciting adventure attractions. It gives visitors a fast, scenic ride above the hills and tea country, making it perfect for thrill seekers and first-time adventure travellers.

What You Can Do

  • Ride the zipline at high speed.
  • Enjoy mountain and tea plantation views.
  • Take photos and videos during your visit.
  • Visit nearby waterfalls and viewpoints in Ella.

Tips

  • Go in the morning to avoid bigger crowds.
  • Wear comfortable clothes and secure footwear.
  • Bring a camera or phone with a secure strap.
  • Check weather conditions before you go.
  • Keep small valuables zipped or in a safe bag.
  • Combine the visit with Little Adam's Peak or Nine Arches Bridge.
