Know Before You Go

Travel Essentials

Key information every traveller should know before arriving in Sri Lanka.

Electricity

230V / 50Hz. Type D & G plug sockets. Bring a universal adaptor.

Currency

Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR). ATMs widely available in cities. USD accepted at tourist spots.

Language

Sinhala & Tamil are official languages. English is widely spoken in tourist areas.

Health

No mandatory vaccinations. Hepatitis A & Typhoid recommended. Tap water not safe to drink.

SIM Cards

Dialog, Mobitel & Airtel offer tourist SIMs at the airport. 4G widely available.

Time Zone

Sri Lanka Standard Time (SLST) - UTC +5:30. No daylight saving time observed.

Dress Respectfully at Temples Always cover your shoulders and knees when visiting Buddhist and Hindu temples. Remove shoes before entering. A sarong is often available to borrow.
Taste the Island

Food & Culture

Sri Lankan cuisine is bold, spiced, and unforgettable. Here are the dishes you absolutely must try.

Hoppers (Appa)

Bowl-shaped rice flour pancakes, eaten at breakfast with sambol or a fried egg inside.

Rice & Curry

The national dish - steamed rice with a vibrant array of curries, dal, and coconut sambol.

Crab Curry

Colombo's famous mud crab cooked in a fiery coconut milk curry. A must at Ministry of Crab.

Kottu Roti

Shredded roti stir-fried with vegetables, egg, and meat on a hot griddle - iconic street food.

Pol Sambol

Fresh coconut relish with red chilli, lime, and Maldive fish - served with almost every meal.

Watalappan

A rich Malay-influenced coconut custard dessert made with jaggery and spices. Heavenly.

Pro Tip: Eat Local Local 'rice and curry' restaurants serve enormous meals for under $2. Skip the tourist menus when you can - the street food is where the real flavour is.

Entry Requirements

Visa & Entry

Most visitors need an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) before arriving. Here's how to get it.

How to Apply for an ETA

1
Visit the Official ETA Portal Go to eta.gov.lk - the only official government website. Beware of scam sites charging extra.
2
Complete the Application Fill in your passport details, travel dates, and accommodation information accurately.
3
Pay the Fee Tourist ETA costs $20 USD (single entry, 30 days). Pay by credit card on the secure portal.
4
Receive Your ETA by Email Usually approved within 24 hours. Print a copy and keep a digital backup on your phone.
5
Extend if Needed Extensions up to 6 months can be applied for at the Department of Immigration in Colombo.
